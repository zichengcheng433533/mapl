Understanding the impact of social media and AI on individuals and society.

From what people know and believe to how they form opinions and take part in civic life. Media, AI, and Public Life (MAPL) is a Vertically Integrated Project team in the School of Journalism at the University of Arizona, where students from different majors work alongside faculty on scientific research projects.

Research

We study how people find information, decide what to trust, form opinions, and take part in public life as more of that happens through feeds, recommendation algorithms, and AI tools. Current work falls into four areas.

Reaching and engaging audiences

How content creators, influencers, and news organizations reach and engage audiences in today's media environment.

Public opinion online

How opinions form and spread in online discussion spaces, and how to tell organic conversation from coordinated activity.

AI in everyday news use

How people, and young adults in particular, use generative AI to find, understand, and check news and public-affairs information.

Digital media and information habits

How people interact with digital media technologies, and how their patterns of information consumption are changing.

Methods

Students learn research methods by working on real projects. No advanced technical skills are needed to start.

  • Survey design and statistical analysis
  • Online experiments
  • Interviews and qualitative analysis
  • Content analysis of news and social media
  • Social media data collection
  • Computational methods

Apply to join

Students from all majors may apply. The team is especially relevant to communication and journalism students, and we also welcome applicants from public relations, marketing, political science, data science, and computer or information science.

What we look for

  • Curiosity about how people use and respond to social media, AI, and news
  • Willingness to learn research methods. Prior research experience is helpful but not required.
  • Attention to detail, clear communication, and reliability with deadlines

Participation

  • Students may join for course credit or as volunteers.
  • New members are accepted at the start of each semester.
  • Fully online students are welcome to apply.

How to apply

Submit your application through the VIP Interest Form and select "Media, AI, and Public Life (MAPL)" as your team.
